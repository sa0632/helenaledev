Privacy Policy

Last updated · August 2026

Overview

This policy covers helenaledev.com, the portfolio site of Helena Le. It explains what information is collected when you visit, and how it's used.

Information Collected

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it. Google Analytics may collect:

  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • General location (city or region level, derived from IP address)
  • Device, browser, and operating system type
  • Referring site or search terms that led you here

No account creation, login, or payment functionality exists on this site, so no such data is collected.

Contact Form Data

If you reach out through the contact section or email directly, the information you provide (such as your name, email address, and message) is used solely to respond to your inquiry. It is not sold, shared, or used for marketing.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish visitors. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, or use a browser extension such as Google's Analytics Opt-out Add-on, without affecting your ability to browse the site.

Third-Party Services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Analytics. Both services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement

Your Choices

You can browse this site without providing any personal information. Analytics tracking can be limited or blocked through browser settings, ad blockers, or opt-out tools without impacting site functionality.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in the site or applicable practices. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
